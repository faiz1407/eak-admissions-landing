Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common queries regarding admissions, documents, and assessments.

What is the admission process for new students? +
The admission process involves submitting an online enquiry, filling out the application form, scheduling an interactive student assessment, and completing final enrollment upon selection.
What documents are required at the time of application? +
You will need the student's birth certificate, previous report cards, passport-size photographs, address proof, and parent ID proofs.
Is there an entrance assessment conducted? +
Yes, we conduct a friendly, age-appropriate interaction and baseline assessment to understand the student's learning level and readiness.
Which classes are currently accepting applications? +
Admissions are currently open for all grades from Nursery up to Grade 12 for the academic session 2027-28.
How can I schedule a campus visit? +
You can click on the "Enquire Now" or "Schedule a Visit" buttons on our website, fill out the quick form, and our admissions team will coordinate a guided campus tour.
